## 0:20 – 0:45 | Block 2: Engineering Hangar & Physics Lock (25s)

**On-Screen Action:**
- `0:20` — Hangar Screen: Select **Light Rocket** ($35M, 1,200 kg max), then click **Veggie Unit** (450 kg) + **DONKI Shield** (500 kg) to trigger the red **Engineering Physics Lock** (`1,300 kg > 1,200 kg`).
- `0:33` — Swap DONKI for **MEDA Station** (350 kg) + **High Relay Orbit**, showing green mass/budget bars (`1,150 / 1,200 kg`, `$75M / $100M` with `$25M` reserve). Click **"Launch Mission to Mars"** and show rocket liftoff animation.

**Narration (EN-US):**
> "Inside the Engineering Hangar, real physics rules every choice. With a 100-million-dollar budget, picking the Light Rocket plus our two heaviest payloads triggers a Mass Overload Physics Lock—1,300 kilograms exceeds the 1,200-kilogram limit! Swapping in the Perseverance MEDA station balances mass, unlocks a Sol-2 reserve bonus, and clears us for liftoff to Mars."

**Legenda Sincronizada (PT-BR):**
> "No Hangar de Engenharia, a física real guia cada escolha. Com orçamento de 100 milhões, combinar o Foguete Leve com as duas cargas mais pesadas ativa a Trava Física por excesso de massa — 1.300 kg superam o limite de 1.200 kg! Trocando para a estação MEDA do Perseverance, equilibramos o peso, garantimos reserva para o Sol 2 e decolamos para Marte."

## 0:45 – 1:25 | Block 3: The 4 Sols, Zero-G Workshop & Commander Quack Chat (40s)

**On-Screen Action:**
- `0:45` — **Sol 1 (Observation):** Show the 4 resource meters, *Biofilms ("Alien Ticks")* quote, and pick Card C (*Local UV Filter*) to open the **Sol 1 Badge Modal** with NASA science explanation.
- `0:57` — **Sol 2 (Hypothesis):** Briefly show the **DONKI Class X8.7** solar flare alert + Hangar Reserve bonus banner, and select Card B.
- `1:05` — **Sol 3 (Experiment — Zero-G Workshop):** Show floating tools in the Ares Base workshop; click 2 hidden tools, hover/open the **"Ask for Help (-8% Life Support)"** modal highlighting teamwork, then grab the 3rd tool to unlock Sol 3 cards.
- `1:16` — Open **Commander Quack Chat** (bottom-right), ask *"Which card should I pick?"* to demonstrate the **anti-spoiler guardrail**, then complete **Sol 4 (Conclusion)** landing.

**Narration (EN-US):**
> "Across four Martian Sols mapped to the Scientific Method, every decision carries real trade-offs. On Sol 1, we fight pipe biofilms—nicknamed 'Alien Ticks'—without waiting 28 minutes for Earth. On Sol 2, we shield against a real DONKI Class X8.7 solar flare. On Sol 3, zero-gravity turbulence scatters tools across the workshop—teaching kids both problem-solving and when it’s okay to ask for help. And if anyone asks Commander Quack for the answer? Our deterministic local AI blocks spoilers and coaches them using live mission telemetry."

**Legenda Sincronizada (PT-BR):**
> "Ao longo de quatro Sóis mapeados no Método Científico, cada decisão traz prós e contras reais. No Sol 1, combatemos biofilmes — apelidados de 'Carrapatos ETs' — sem esperar 28 minutos pela Terra. No Sol 2, enfrentamos uma explosão solar real Classe X8.7 do DONKI. No Sol 3, a microgravidade espalha ferramentas pela oficina — ensinando resolução de problemas e a importância de pedir ajuda. E se alguém pedir a resposta ao Comandante Quack? Nosso agente local bloqueia spoilers e orienta usando a telemetria da missão."
